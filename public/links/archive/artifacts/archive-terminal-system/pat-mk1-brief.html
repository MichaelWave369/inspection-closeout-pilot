PARALLAX ARCHIVE · CREATION SHELL

PAT Mk.1

The first archive instrument designed entirely from Parallax principles rather than borrowed historical identity.

Operational boundary: PAT Mk.1 is a public archive interface. Its governance language explains records and limitations; it is not presented as an autonomous authority or hidden decision system.

Original identity

Fieldglass, Office Keys, the Ledger Dial, Witness Light, Graph Cinema, and a permanent Dissent rail form a coherent machine language built around continuity and human authority.

Field mode

Field presents project identity, story, evidence, current state, and next trajectory. It is the fastest path from the machine into a canonical project record.

Graph mode

Graph Cinema turns typed Archive Core relationships into navigable motion. Explicit relationships are used when available; same-domain links are clearly labeled as contextual fallbacks.

Ledger mode

Ledger exposes current status, visible evidence, artifact states, archive position, and the next proof gate. It keeps design-stage work visibly separate from published or validated artifacts.

Dissent mode

Every project receives a visible limitation channel. Rich records expose explicit claim boundaries; thinner records receive a safe generic boundary rather than invented criticism.

Witness Light

The Witness Light reports load, active, and failure states while the machine states its privacy contract: public presentation, no account requirement, and no tracking dependency.
