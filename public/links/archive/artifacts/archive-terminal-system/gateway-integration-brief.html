Archive Gateway Integration

The public entry contract connecting the physical business card, contact page, Archive Gateway, six terminal shells, and accessible Network fallback.

Boundary: this brief summarizes the canonical integration specification. The complete DOCX remains the layout-authoritative source.

Purpose

The Gateway turns the business card into an access credential rather than a decorative QR endpoint. A visitor scans the card, reaches Michael W. Hughes’ professional contact page, chooses Enter Parallax Network, and arrives at a short skippable activation sequence before selecting a terminal.

Canonical route

The contact page links to the Archive Gateway with an explicit card-entry state. Direct visitors may bypass activation, while card-origin visitors receive the full access-token moment. The conventional Network page remains available at every stage as the semantic and accessible fallback.

State machine

The experience separates card verification, activation, terminal selection, shell entry, direct project records, and fallback browsing. Each transition has a stable URL and must remain recoverable through browser navigation.

Accessibility

Activation is skippable. Reduced-motion visitors do not wait through staged effects. Terminal cards use real links, direct project records remain independent of immersive shells, and JavaScript failure never blocks access to the standard Network.

Performance and privacy

The Gateway contains no account requirement, analytics dependency, autoplay audio, or hidden identity inference. Core HTML is usable before effects. Terminal visuals progressively enhance the route rather than controlling it.

Operational rule

The business card, contact page, Gateway, shells, Archive Core, and Network fallback form one public path. A change to any link must be tested across the complete journey rather than reviewed as an isolated page.
