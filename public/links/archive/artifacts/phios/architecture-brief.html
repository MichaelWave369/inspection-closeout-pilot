SOVEREIGN OPERATOR SHELL · LOCAL-FIRST · MANUAL PROMOTION

Give the operator a coherent shell without replacing the runtime core—or the human.

PhiOS is the sovereign operator shell above PhiKernel. PhiKernel remains the authoritative runtime core for its declared internal objects and safety contracts; PhiOS renders operator-facing views, composes workflows, exposes bounded interfaces, and preserves reviewable local artifacts.

Architecture boundary: PhiKernel is a source of truth inside the software runtime. It is not a source of truth about a human being, consciousness, psychology, health, biology, or physical law.
Linux → PhiKernel runtime core → stable CLI / normalized contract → PhiOS operator shell → local reports, sessions, archives, and bounded interfaces

Loose-coupled runtime relationship

PhiKernel owns runtime truth

The repository assigns anchor, capsules, heart, coherence, routing safety, and normalized runtime behavior to PhiKernel rather than duplicating those internals in PhiOS.

PhiOS owns operator experience

PhiOS calls stable interfaces, re-renders operator-friendly output, composes workflows, creates local review artifacts, and surfaces the controls needed to inspect and act deliberately.

Optional adapter integration

Kernel integration is default-off. A primary adapter may be paired with an optional shadow adapter for compare-mode analysis without changing primary behavior.

Manual promotion

Compare records and readiness states support operator review. They never auto-promote an adapter; promotion, reverse-shadow validation, and rollback remain deliberate actions.

Context and continuity role

Within the Agentora integration lineage, PhiOS owns identity and persona context packets, memory-context preparation, and writeback. Those packets should carry declared labels, source pointers, timestamps, relevance, scope, uncertainty, conflict state, privacy classification, expiry, and the operator’s authority decision.

Identity and persona fields are collaboration context. They do not establish legal identity, biological identity, personhood, sentience, consciousness, or a fixed psychological profile. Memory entries are retrieved records—not unquestionable facts—and writeback is a proposed continuity event that may be corrected, narrowed, declined, or deleted.

MCP and capability surfaces

PhiOS exposes an additive MCP interface over existing PhiOS and PhiKernel capabilities. The repository documents read-only resources, bounded summary tools, local history and observatory browsing, sessions, archive navigation, optional pulse and dispatch capabilities, and profile presets such as read-only, observer, operator, and developer. Capability gating is described as local and lightweight—not a complete identity or authentication platform.

Observatory and archive layers

PhiOS includes extensive local observatory, archive, learning, storyboard, dossier, field-library, shelf, reading-room, study-hall, pathway, map, catalog, and dashboard surfaces. These layers organize and interpret local artifacts. The repository repeatedly states that they are non-truth-bearing interpretive or curation layers and do not alter PhiKernel truth logic.

Internal metrics and scientific boundaries

Terms such as field, heart, coherence, anchor, pulse, theoretical attractor, bio-band, and fitness are internal system objects, labels, calculations, or exploratory framing. They are not validated measures of mental health, consciousness, human worth, diagnosis, treatment, biological state, or established physical law. The repository itself marks some scientific framings as structural, experimental, or unconfirmed.
