CARBON LOOP · HUMAN EXPERIENCE OS · LUMENBRIDGE

A reflection layer that returns authority to the person.

Carbon Loop is the canonical project. Human Experience OS names its architectural lineage. LumenBridge is the public-facing reflection experience. Coherence Bridge provides safety, continuity, drift detection, and human-handoff principles within the larger system.

Boundary: Carbon Loop is not therapy, diagnosis, treatment, crisis care, or a replacement for relationships. Its purpose is to help a person clarify what is happening, identify what matters, choose one meaningful next action, and reconnect with real-world support when useful.
Arrive → Name → Notice what matters → Choose → Act → Reconnect → Continue only with consent

Design purpose

Many digital systems optimize for retention, dependence, and endless interaction. Carbon Loop defines a different success condition: the person leaves with more clarity, stronger agency, a smaller next step, and a better connection to their own life. More screen time is not the goal.

System layers

Carbon Loop

The canonical human-centered project and governing design frame.

Human Experience OS lineage

The architecture language for reflection, continuity, consent, context, and meaningful action.

LumenBridge

The public experience: simple prompts, explicit boundaries, local-first operation, and an easy return to the world beyond the screen.

Coherence Bridge

The safety and continuity subsystem: calibration, drift awareness, protected dissent, real-person handoff, and limits on authority.

Local-first continuity

Reflection should work without an account or remote profile. A temporary session is the default. Any future continuity feature must be opt-in, visible, inspectable, exportable, revocable, and deletable. The system must not silently infer a permanent identity from vulnerable moments.

Human authority

The person retains the right to pause, skip, correct, delete, exit, or share. A Computational Collaborator may help organize language or prepare a handoff, but may not declare what the person “really feels,” make clinical judgments, manufacture intimacy, or extend its mandate without renewal.

First public proof

The first LumenBridge prototype is intentionally fixed and local. It uses no model calls, stores no history, produces no score, and makes no diagnosis. It demonstrates the reflection rhythm and human-handoff boundary without pretending the full Carbon Loop runtime is complete.

Current claim boundary: the published work demonstrates architecture, language, consent controls, and a bounded browser interaction. It does not establish clinical benefit, crisis effectiveness, long-term behavior change, privacy certification, or safe use with vulnerable populations.
