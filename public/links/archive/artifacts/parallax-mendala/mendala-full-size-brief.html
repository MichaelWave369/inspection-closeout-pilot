PARALLAX MENDALA · FULL-SIZE FAMILY

Living agriculture as an observable system.

MENDALA expands to “Modular Ecological Nutrient Delivery & Autonomous Living Agriculture.” The full-size architecture organizes water, light, air, roots, data, maintenance, and human authority into a modular circular food-production platform.

Boundary: this is a curated public brief derived from the retained master specification. It is a design basis for prototyping and costed engineering—not evidence of validated yield, durability, or commercial readiness.

Product family

MENDALA-24

Compact fixed entry configuration for constrained pilot environments.

MENDALA-48

The first fixed pilot recommended after the water architecture is proven.

MENDALA-108 Community Foodwheel

The reference configuration described by the canonical master specification.

MENDALA-216

A larger future community-scale configuration, still downstream of smaller proofs.

System pattern

The six-petal architecture is not merely decorative. It separates essential domains so each can be inspected and repaired: water circulation, lighting, air and climate, root-zone health, sensing and data, and operational cycle. The central operator layer preserves manual override and prevents automation from becoming an unexplained black box.

Proof sequence

  1. Build a working dual-loop water bench and measure flow, leaks, temperature, conditioning, uptime, and maintenance.
  2. Use those receipts to build a MENDALA-48 fixed pilot.
  3. Only after crop cycles are measured should larger-system output or cost claims be revised.
Current claim boundary: the architecture is established at concept and specification level. No public statement should imply that MENDALA-108 or MENDALA-216 has been physically validated.
