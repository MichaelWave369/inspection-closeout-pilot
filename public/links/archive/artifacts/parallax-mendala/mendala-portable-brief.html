PORTABLE FAMILY · HOUSEHOLD / CLASSROOM / RV / DEMO / EMERGENCY

The six-petal architecture, made portable.

The portable family carries the same observable MENDALA pattern into smaller systems intended for learning, household use, mobile demonstrations, RV environments, and emergency-resilience experiments.

Reference prototype: Go-18 is identified by the canonical portable specification as the first serious prototype.

Portable models

Seed-6

A small introductory system for learning, seedlings, classrooms, and compact household experiments.

Go-18

The reference portable prototype and first serious build target.

Go XL-36

A larger mobile or demonstration-scale system for expanded growing and resilience scenarios.

Shared architecture

Six functional domains, accessible computing, environmental sensing, inspectable circulation, and human override.

Why Go-18 comes first

Go-18 is large enough to expose real integration problems—water handling, sensing, lighting, maintenance, enclosure, portability, and operator workflow—without carrying the cost and complexity of a community-scale system. It is therefore the most useful bridge between concept documents and physical evidence.

Prototype receipts

The first build should publish measured uptime, leak events, water and energy use, maintenance time, environmental stability, crop type, cycle duration, harvested mass, failures, repairs, and all relevant test conditions.

Current claim boundary: portable cost and production figures are planning assumptions until the Go-18 prototype is built and instrumented. Emergency-use language describes an intended role, not certified emergency equipment.
