COST PLANNING · EARLY PROTOTYPE RANGE

Useful ranges, without false precision.

These figures are transparent early planning bands from the portable specification. They are not retail prices, bids, manufacturing quotes, or promises of final build cost.

Documented planning bands

Introductory compact system.

Go-18 · natural light$900–$1,800

Reference prototype including indoor-lighting requirements.

Go XL-36$1,800–$4,800

Larger mobile or demonstration-scale configuration.

Why ranges are wide

Early cost depends on enclosure method, lighting, pumps, sensors, reservoirs, control hardware, materials, fabrication approach, local sourcing, shipping, safety components, redundancy, and whether labor is counted. Prototype costs also include learning and rework that may not persist in a mature design.

What is not included here

This public brief does not publish model-by-model yield or monthly-output claims. Those figures require a reviewed assumptions table and, ultimately, measured prototype results. The archive will not convert design enthusiasm into implied agricultural performance.

Use rule: cite these figures only as dated planning bands from the v0.1 portable specification. Do not describe them as “the cost” of a finished MENDALA product.
