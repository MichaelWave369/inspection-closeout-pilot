CLAIMS · EVENTS · AUTHORITY · CUSTODY · REPAIR

The receipt layer beneath Parallax.

AlphaLedgerOmega is a family of evidence ledgers designed to preserve what was claimed, which source was used, who or what acted, what authority applied, what changed, what remains uncertain, and what repair is still required.

Boundary: ALO does not manufacture truth. It preserves inspectable evidence continuity so reviewers can distinguish complete records from strong evidence—and both from validated claims.
Observation → Source → Event → Authority → Action → Receipt → Verification → Claim → Repair

One grammar, specialized families

Different domains need different evidence. A protein observation, atomic calculation, circuit execution, environmental test, human-consent event, and data-center custody record cannot be treated as interchangeable. ALO therefore uses specialized families with a shared receipt envelope.

Proteo

Protein and biological evidence structures.

Element

Chemical-element identity, composition, and evidence.

Atom

Atomic-scale models, calculations, and observations.

Circuit

Software, hardware, compute execution, and governed rewrites.

Neuro

Human-context continuity and consent events, without clinical inference.

Material

Materials, fabrication, provenance, and physical-condition evidence.

Spatial

Location, geometry, boundaries, and physical configuration.

Energy

Power, thermal, environmental, and instrumented system tests.

DataCenter

Infrastructure, deployment, artifact custody, and operational continuity.

Shared receipt envelope

Every family should preserve receipt identity, schema, actor, subject, source, custody, authority, event, before-and-after state, supported claims, unsupported claims, uncertainty, dissent, integrity metadata, supersession, and repair conditions.

Correction rather than erasure

A ledger should not hide an earlier mistake by silently rewriting history. Corrections attach a successor receipt that explains what changed and why. Revocation, deletion, supersession, and repair each need explicit events appropriate to their domain.

First public proof

The ALO Receipt Inspector loads four synthetic demonstration receipts and checks their exact bytes against a SHA-256 manifest. The fixtures show how MENDALA, Graph Cinema, Carbon Loop, and the Archive can share one envelope while retaining different evidence boundaries.

Current claim boundary: the public package demonstrates a receipt schema, fixture-manifest integrity check, claim-boundary presentation, and cross-project evidence pattern. It does not prove real-world events, cryptographic identity, tamper-proof storage, independent verification, legal admissibility, or the truth of fixture statements.
