This demonstration loads fixed AlphaLedgerOmega receipts from the public artifact package, calculates their SHA-256 digests in your browser, and compares them with an external manifest.

A matching digest means only that the loaded bytes match the registered fixture. It does not prove who authored the record, whether the source was honest, whether the method was sound, or whether the described event occurred.
