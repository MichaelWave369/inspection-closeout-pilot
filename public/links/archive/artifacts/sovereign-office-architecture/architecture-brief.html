SEPARATED OFFICES · PROTECTED DISSENT · HUMAN AUTHORITY

No single intelligence should observe, judge, authorize, execute, and excuse itself.

Sovereign Office Architecture separates critical functions into Observer, Cartographer, Protected Dissenter, Governor, Operator, and Repairer so decisions can be inspected as a chain of distinct judgments rather than one system validating its own output.

Boundary: office names and separated prompts do not automatically prove independence. Shared models, training data, retrieval, operators, incentives, or infrastructure can still create correlated failure.
Decision packet → sealed judgments → synthesis with dissent → human authority → bounded operation → receipt → repair

The six offices

Observer

Reports stated facts, sources, missing evidence, and uncertainty without creating authority.

Cartographer

Maps dependencies, consequences, alternate paths, constraints, and affected systems.

Protected Dissenter

Challenges consensus, weak evidence, hidden costs, stale mandates, and self-serving synthesis. Its objection remains attached.

Governor

Checks admissibility, human mandate, authority scope, stop conditions, and whether action is permitted.

Operator

Executes only the authorized action envelope and records what changed, failed, or was refused.

Repairer

Restores invariants, proposes rollback, documents repair, and preserves the original failure and receipts.

Sealed judgments

Each office should produce its judgment before seeing a synthesis that could bias it toward agreement. Sealing does not make a judgment true; it makes the timing, scope, authorship, and later modification of that judgment inspectable.

Human authority

Office agreement may support a decision, but it does not manufacture a mandate. The authorized human or accountable institution defines purpose, scope, data access, action limits, renewal, and stop conditions. The Governor checks that authority; it does not invent it.

v1.2 and v1.3 lineage

The retained SOSA canon includes a v1.2 final baseline and v1.3 alpha work covering Bridge Runner, Operator Authority, Signed Envelopes, and Freeze Drift. This public package summarizes those ideas without claiming a complete production runtime or validated multi-model independence.

Freeze Drift

Material changes in scope, retained context, office mandate, execution rights, or authority require a new decision packet and receipt. Quietly expanding a mandate because a system has been useful is treated as drift, not consent.

Claim boundary: this package demonstrates governance architecture, fixed synthetic office judgments, public explanation, and Archive integration. It does not prove independent reasoning, collusion resistance, correct decisions, legal authority, secure signatures, safe autonomous operation, or reliable behavior under real-world pressure.
