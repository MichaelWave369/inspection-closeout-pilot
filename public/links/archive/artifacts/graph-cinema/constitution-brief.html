Canonical motion path

Protected dissent

Dissent is not decorative commentary. A protected objection must remain attached to the decision path even when the authorized trajectory proceeds. The viewer may summarize it, but must preserve access to the original judgment and its receipt identity.

Constitutional rule

Admissible motion is first-class. Canonical state changes require validated events, authority checks, an accepted decision object, a bounded rewrite, and a complete receipt. Graph Cinema is downstream of that process.

Current claim boundary: this brief explains a constitutional design baseline. It is not evidence that all future implementations preserve office independence, resist collusion, or produce correct governance outcomes.
