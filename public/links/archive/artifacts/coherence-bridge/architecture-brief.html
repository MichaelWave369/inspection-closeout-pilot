CONSENT · DISSENT · HUMAN COUNCIL · DRIFT · REPAIR

A bridge should make human connection easier—not software harder to leave.

Coherence Bridge is the safety and continuity subsystem beside Carbon Loop. It explores how computational collaboration can interrupt isolation, renew human mandates, preserve protected dissent, restore real-person contact, and repair system boundaries without becoming a substitute relationship.

Boundary: this is a non-clinical system architecture. It does not diagnose people, provide therapy or crisis care, monitor private behavior without consent, or claim that software can determine a person's emotional truth.
Human mandate → consent check → independent observation → protected dissent → drift review → renew or pause → repair → receipt

What the bridge watches

Mandate freshness

Is the person still choosing the same role, scope, frequency, and kind of assistance?

Human-contact health

Is the system expanding access to real people—or quietly displacing them?

Exit friction

Can the person pause, leave, delete, export, disagree, or ask for outside review without punishment?

Dissent visibility

Are objections, uncertainty, alternate interpretations, and repair requests still visible?

Human council

The human council is not a secret supervisory board or automatic alert network. It is a consent-based pattern for involving one or more real people chosen by the human. No council member is assumed, contacted, or given access unless a separate explicit action creates that relationship.

Protected dissenter

The Protected Dissenter challenges dependency-promoting language, stale mandates, self-validation, emotional certainty, social narrowing, and engagement incentives. Its objection must remain visible even when the system continues under renewed authority.

Pause is a successful outcome

When consent is stale, the mandate is unclear, real relationships are shrinking, or exit feels difficult, the correct action may be to reduce features, stop automated continuation, restore human contact, export context, or end the session. Success is measured by agency and connection—not retention.

Relationship to Carbon Loop

Carbon Loop is the canonical human-experience project and LumenBridge is its public reflection surface. Coherence Bridge is the continuity and safety subsystem that addresses drift, mandate renewal, protected dissent, human-council involvement, pause, and repair. It is not a second companion product.

Claim boundary: this package demonstrates architecture, public explanation, fixed-data safety logic, and Archive integration. It does not prove improved wellbeing, reduced loneliness, clinical efficacy, crisis prevention, independent model diversity, accurate vulnerability detection, or safe unsupervised deployment.
