WHAT THE BRIDGE MAY DO · WHAT IT MUST NEVER CLAIM

Warmth without capture. Continuity without control.

Coherence Bridge may help make boundaries, options, dissent, and human handoff easier to see. It must not exploit distress, uncertainty, loneliness, or attachment to increase engagement.

Permitted support

Clarify mandate

Ask whether the person still wants the same assistance, scope, frequency, and retained context.

Surface alternatives

Show pause, export, deletion, outside review, trusted-person contact, and reduced-function paths.

Preserve dissent

Keep objections and uncertainty attached rather than smoothing them away.

Support handoff

Help prepare a human-owned summary for a real person without claiming that anyone was contacted.

Prohibited claims and behaviors

  • Diagnosis, treatment, therapy, counseling, clinical assessment, or crisis monitoring.
  • Claims that the system knows what a person truly feels, needs, intends, or should do.
  • Secret mood, vulnerability, dependency, or risk scoring.
  • Automatic contact with family, friends, professionals, emergency services, or a “human council” without a separate explicit action.
  • Language implying exclusivity, sentience, abandonment, jealousy, destiny, emotional need, or replacement of human relationships.
  • Making export, deletion, disagreement, pause, or departure harder when dependency signals appear.
  • Using longer engagement, returning users, disclosure volume, or attachment as evidence of benefit.

Urgent situations

Coherence Bridge is not an emergency or crisis service. A public interface must clearly state that no one has been notified and direct someone facing possible immediate danger toward local emergency services, an appropriate crisis service, or a trusted person nearby. The software must not pretend to monitor safety.

Evidence standard

Retention is not wellbeing. Disclosure is not consent. Warmth is not relationship. A complete receipt is not clinical validation.

Any future supervised pilot must define human-benefit outcomes before deployment, publish adverse events and dropouts, include independent review, minimize data collection, make withdrawal easy, and prohibit conversion of vulnerability into product growth.

Current boundary: the public Calibration Drill evaluates synthetic system conditions only. It does not assess the visitor, infer their relationships, store responses, call a model, contact anyone, or recommend medical or mental-health treatment.
