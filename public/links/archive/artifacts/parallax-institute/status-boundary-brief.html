Not claimed by this package

  • Incorporation, nonprofit registration, tax-exempt status, or tax-deductible contributions.
  • Accreditation, degrees, professional licensure, clinical services, or recognized academic authority.
  • Ownership or control of land, a campus, laboratories, housing, utilities, or public infrastructure.
  • Governmental, regulatory, grantmaking, certifying, or enforcement power.
  • That every Parallax project has achieved net-positive human or ecological impact.
  • That collaborators, users, or supporters acquire ownership or governing authority by participating.

Public language rule

Say what exists. Name what is proposed. Show the evidence. Preserve uncertainty. Publish the repair path.

Words such as institute, laboratory, office, council, or school describe system roles only when their current status is clearly disclosed. They must not be used to imply credentials, legal powers, facilities, or independent validation that do not exist.

Authority rule

The founding vision remains under Michael W. Hughes's authority unless a specific mandate says otherwise. Advice, critique, code, testing, partnership, or public participation does not create an automatic ownership interest or open-ended mandate.

Boundary: this page is an institutional disclosure, not legal, tax, financial, educational, medical, real-estate, or accreditation advice. Future status changes require direct evidence and an updated Archive receipt.
