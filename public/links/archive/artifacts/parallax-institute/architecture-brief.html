STEWARDSHIP · RESEARCH · PROTOTYPING · PUBLIC BENEFIT

A home for work that must remain understandable, bounded, and useful.

Parallax Institute is the public institutional identity connecting Parallax research, software, physical invention, human-centered systems, infrastructure, education, and creative worlds. It gives the work a shared stewardship frame without collapsing every project into one undifferentiated brand.

Current status: the Institute is an active public identity and architectural framework. It is not presently represented here as an incorporated entity, accredited school, tax-exempt charity, grantmaking body, regulator, or operating physical campus.
Imagination → Bounded research → Prototype → Evidence → Repair → Public benefit

Four institutional functions

Stewardship

Preserve intent, source lineage, claim boundaries, consent, authority, continuity, and the right to repair.

Research

Separate hypotheses, formal models, experiments, observations, and conclusions so speculation cannot silently become fact.

Prototyping

Move ideas into inspectable software, physical systems, simulations, fixtures, and field trials with bounded claims.

Public benefit

Return human, ecological, knowledge, resilience, or livelihood value rather than optimizing only for extraction or attention.

Public ecosystem

EnterTheField.org is the intended public doorway and commons. The Parallax Archive preserves projects, artifacts, receipts, and lineage. PHI369 Labs names the experimental research-and-building lane. The Institute holds stewardship, teaching, covenant, and public explanation. Creative worlds translate the deeper principles into music, games, stories, and experiences.

Regenerative sector covenant

Every active sector should return more verified value than it consumes and should not hide harm in another department, community, ecosystem, or future accounting period. The covenant is a governing commitment. It does not imply that every current project has already demonstrated net-positive impact.

Vision and control

Collaboration may contribute evidence, critique, labor, testing, or partnership, but participation does not automatically confer ownership, authority, or the right to redirect the Institute's founding vision. Authority must be explicit, bounded, and receipted.

Physical future

The Mt. Shasta / Pilgrim Creek Institute remains a future physical vision for teaching, research, healing arts, land stewardship, infrastructure, culture, and regional service. This public brief does not state that land, facilities, permits, financing, staffing, or operational capacity have been secured.

Claim boundary: this package demonstrates an institutional architecture, public explanation, covenant, and live Archive integration. It does not establish legal status, accreditation, tax treatment, charitable eligibility, physical-campus readiness, measured regenerative impact, or authority over independent institutions.
