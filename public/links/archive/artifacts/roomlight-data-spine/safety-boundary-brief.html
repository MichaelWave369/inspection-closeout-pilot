OBSERVATION IS NOT CERTIFICATION

A complete packet can still contain a bad reading.

RoomLight must preserve the difference between sensor output, interpreted condition, human decision, authorized action, and verified field evidence.

RoomLight may

  • Read identified local sensors under stated conditions.
  • Record source, unit, timestamp, location scope, calibration state, packet freshness, and uncertainty.
  • Surface missing data, disagreement, drift, and out-of-range observations for review.
  • Quarantine uncertain packets and create demonstration or field receipts with visible limits.
  • Support separately authorized, bounded actions where appropriate engineering controls exist.

RoomLight may not claim by default

  • That a room, building, workplace, grow environment, or outdoor area is safe.
  • Certified accuracy, regulatory compliance, laboratory equivalence, or professional inspection.
  • Medical diagnosis, health risk determination, emergency detection, or crisis response.
  • That one threshold crossing proves a hazard, cause, or required intervention.
  • Permission to start, stop, energize, ventilate, dose, heat, cool, pump, unlock, or otherwise control hazardous equipment.
  • That a well-formed packet proves the sensor, installation, method, operator, or underlying claim was correct.

Quarantine rule

Stale · missing · disagreeing · uncalibrated · implausible → quarantine and review

The system should not hide uncertainty through averaging, smoothing, confidence language, or silent substitution. A quarantined packet remains useful evidence of a measurement problem.

Authority rule

Observation does not create control authority. Any actuator path requires a separate mandate, explicit scope, human override, fail-safe state, equipment-specific review, and a receipt linking the action to its authority.

Privacy rule

Local-first means collection remains bounded and visible. Sensor placement, retention, export, sharing, and deletion must be disclosed. The public Field Bench uses fixed synthetic data, connects to no live sensor, stores nothing, and sends nothing.

Safety boundary: RoomLight is not an emergency service, certified safety system, medical device, or replacement for qualified inspection. When life safety, code compliance, health, hazardous equipment, or regulated environments are involved, use appropriate certified instruments and qualified professionals.
