LOCAL SENSING · CALIBRATION · SAFETY · RECEIPTS

Observe locally. Preserve uncertainty. Let humans decide.

RoomLight Data Spine is a local-first environmental sensing architecture designed to make sensor identity, calibration state, packet freshness, uncertainty, human authority, and field receipts visible.

Boundary: RoomLight is not a certified instrument, medical device, emergency service, or environmental safety guarantee. A reading may support review; it does not establish that a space is safe.
Sensor → local packet → quality gate → safety envelope → human review → receipt

System layers

Room node

Collects stated measurements from identified sensors and records units, timestamp, location scope, and source identity.

Calibration and freshness

Tracks calibration age, expected range, packet age, missing data, and disagreements between sensors.

Safety envelope

Separates observation from advice and control. Uncertain packets may be quarantined rather than converted into confident output.

Human review and receipt

A person may accept, reject, request recalibration, pause downstream action, or document a bounded decision.

Starter-firmware lineage

The retained RoomLight source canon includes starter firmware and the v0.2.2 safety patch lineage. Those materials support the existence of a firmware and safety-design path; they do not by themselves prove long-term field reliability, sensor accuracy, cybersecurity, or a monitored production installation.

Observation before automation

The public architecture begins with inspectable observation. Any later actuator path must use separate authority, a bounded action envelope, manual override, fail-safe behavior, and domain-appropriate engineering review. RoomLight should never infer permission to control hazardous equipment merely because a sensor value crossed a threshold.

Evidence path

The next physical proof is an instrumented starter installation with known sensor models, stated calibration methods, local packet logs, outages, drift, disagreements, manual interventions, maintenance, and signed field receipts.

Claim boundary: this package demonstrates architecture, public explanation, synthetic packet logic, and Archive integration. It does not prove live environmental monitoring, certified accuracy, code compliance, indoor-air safety, medical relevance, emergency response, secure deployment, or authorized equipment control.
