WHAT THE REGISTRY MAY KNOW · WHAT IT MAY NOT CLAIM

A registry should reduce confusion without expanding surveillance.

Parallax Census separates local discovery, candidate classification, public permission, canonical admission, and later correction so that useful organization does not become silent appropriation.

Permitted public purpose

The public Census may organize approved records about Parallax projects, artifacts, source lineage, aliases, relationships, publication states, and evidence paths. It may expose uncertainty, missing fields, duplicate risk, and the action required before stronger claims are allowed.

Not a people census

  • No population enumeration, identity profiling, relationship mapping, hidden behavioral tracking, vulnerability scoring, or biometric inference.
  • No automatic collection of contacts, conversations, location histories, private files, or account activity.
  • No assumption that a named person consented to public inclusion merely because their name appears in a source.

Discovery does not establish

Ownership

Finding a file, URL, image, name, or repository does not establish title, authorship, license, or right to redistribute.

Truth

Repeated or internally consistent metadata can still describe a false, stale, incomplete, or misunderstood claim.

Consent

Possession and access are not permission to expose private material or identify another person publicly.

Uniqueness

Matching titles can describe different projects; different titles can describe the same lineage. Deduplication requires evidence and review.

Quarantine before confidence

Private sources, missing custody, ambiguous aliases, conflicting claims, stale records, and artifacts without public permission remain quarantined or locally retained. Quarantine is not a judgment that the material is false; it is a statement that public canonical admission is not yet justified.

Withdrawal and correction

Authorized people must have a clear path to challenge attribution, request correction, narrow public fields, withdraw an artifact from display, or attach dissent. The registry should preserve only the minimum continuity record permitted by the governing authority and consent boundary.

No automatic publication

Eligible for review ≠ approved ≠ published ≠ proven true

The public demo cannot mutate Archive Core. Any real admission requires an explicit human decision, a versioned change, a custody receipt, and a deployment review.

Boundary: this package is not legal advice, copyright clearance, a rights registry, an identity database, a complete inventory, or proof that omitted work does not exist. No real person or private source is assessed by the Registry Workbench.
