WHAT A PROOF RUN MAY TEST · WHAT IT MAY NOT CLAIM

The first proof is allowed to stop, fail safely, or say not yet.

SignalBridge separates planning evidence from live execution so a persuasive migration story cannot become an unauthorized operational command.

Mock/no-live-action first

The initial proof mode uses synthetic or frozen data, shadow comparison, explicit assumptions, and no production side effects. It may test whether a workflow is sufficiently defined, observable, reversible, and receiptable to justify a later authorized pilot.

Readiness is not certification

Score

A bounded planning heuristic across process, provenance, authority, reversibility, evidence, and exception handling.

Blocker

A single unresolved custody, privacy, authority, safety, or rollback problem may override a high score.

Recommendation

Not yet, shadow trial only, human-gated run, or bounded Proof Run candidate.

Decision

Only an authorized human process may admit, modify, or reject the candidate.

Economic delta boundary

Any displayed savings, consolidation opportunity, time reduction, or cost range is a hypothesis based on declared source data. It is not audited finance, a purchasing recommendation, a valuation, a guarantee, or proof that a tool can be removed without harming the outcome.

Required handoff fields

Intent · actor · authority · boundary declaration · allowed/prohibited actions · evidence · exception path · rollback/compensation · signature

A handoff manifest is complete enough to review only when the receiving party can understand what is proposed, who may decide, what cannot happen, what evidence must be produced, how exceptions escalate, and how the change can be reversed or compensated.

What this public workbench cannot do

  • It cannot inspect a real organization, upload CSV files, call external services, authenticate accounts, contact vendors, move data, cancel software, purchase software, alter contracts, or execute workflows.
  • It cannot establish production traction, revenue, verified savings, rights to access data, organizational consent, implementation reliability, or automated-underwriting validity.
  • It cannot sign a manifest, create a live execution contract, issue authority, or mutate Archive Core.

Proof Run receipts

A future real Proof Run should preserve the frozen input, assumptions, authority, allowed and prohibited actions, expected outcome, observed result, exceptions, evidence pointers, human decisions, rollback events, and settlement. A failed or rejected trial remains useful evidence and must not disappear from the record.

Boundary: every output in the public workbench is synthetic, unsigned, demonstration-only, non-persistent, and non-canonical. “Proof Run candidate” means eligible for further human review—not safe, approved, implemented, profitable, or ready for production.
